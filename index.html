Stewie

Stewie is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Stewie reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar with read-only access. It cannot send, change or delete emails or events.

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
